Training & presentations

Sessions on technical communication practices, documentation standards, and content strategy — delivered to internal teams and cross-functional audiences.

Training

DITA fundamentals for the TechCom team

A session delivered to the SLB TechCom department covering topic types (task, concept, reference), ditamaps, content reuse via conref, and conditional publishing. Designed as an onboarding module for writers new to structured authoring.

Training

Simplified Technical English (STE) workshop

A practical workshop on writing with controlled language rules. Covers the STE specification, approved word lists, writing rules for procedures versus descriptions, and before/after rewrites from actual equipment manuals.

Presentation

AI-assisted documentation workflows

A presentation on integrating AI tools into the documentation lifecycle — from prompt frameworks for first-draft generation to review processes that maintain accuracy and voice. Includes examples from the Seismic documentation pipeline.

Presentation

Content strategy for multi-product platforms

A talk on structuring documentation across a multi-product SaaS platform — how to handle shared concepts, feature-specific content, and cross-product navigation when the information architecture spans several product lines.

Training

Plain language editing for engineers

A session designed for engineering teams contributing to documentation. Covers sentence-level editing techniques, active voice, front-loading key information, and the difference between writing for developers versus writing for end users.
